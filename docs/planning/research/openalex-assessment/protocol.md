# Assessment evidence protocol

Date: 2026-10-04. Target: short required manager review after the on-platform interview in HR Vision.

Status: targeted evidence scan, not systematic review, no saturation claim.

Budget checked first: OpenAlex anonymous mode, reported $0.10 available (1000 credits), no API key. Budget target: under $0.02, max 6 count probes, 6 searches, one small snowball expansion. No identifying or candidate data sent.

Concepts: structured employment interview; behaviorally anchored ratings / BARS; independent interview judgments; note-taking / evidence; response burden / questionnaire length; explicit missing evidence. Product constraint: at most 3–4 required prompts on the first screen.

Inclusion criteria fixed before abstracts: original peer-reviewed empirical employment-interview studies or original meta-analysis; directly addresses rating structure, predictive/criterion validity, bias, evidence recording, or form burden. Official component documentation is admissible only for implementation. Prefer DOI and readable publisher/institutional abstract/full text. Exclude unverified marketing, vendor candidate-score claims, irrelevant clinical interviews, and articles marked retracted.

Selection: do not use citation count as quality or validity score. Note design/population and separate empirical finding from HR Vision inference. A short UI is a design hypothesis; three prompts are not a science-backed universal optimum. Candidate self-report, observed behavior, human interpretation, and later work outcomes remain distinct.

Collection: keyword search, semantic attempt, citation snowball. Preserve exact commands and errors. Verify every scientific DOI cited in final report with oa.py verify. If any channel fails, report it and continue with available primary sources.
